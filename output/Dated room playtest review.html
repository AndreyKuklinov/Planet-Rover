Dated room playtest review

20 original rooms played, 10 revised, 10 retained, and 10 added. Both passes used the rover controls in Unity Play Mode. Each route finished with all beds planted, without reset.

Steps count scripted input groups. Simultaneous hands count as one group, and an explicit wait for a door also counts as one. These are completed routes in authored orientation. Human completion times, alternate solutions, and multiplayer pacing were not measured.

Dots are seeds, outlined squares are beds, and the diamond is the rover. A tilde marks a dye cloud. L, D, and T mark the lever, door, and timer.

001 · Bent rail

Retained · 6 input steps

Two pickups sit to either side of the rover. The beds ask for a change of row. A readable introduction, with little reason to revisit once the arm directions are familiar.

002 · Pick a gate

Retained · 8 input steps

The five teal seeds are also five possible doors. Choosing one to pick up determines where the rover can cross to the three beds. Two seeds can stay in the fence.

003 · Break the seal

Retained · 19 input steps

The rover begins inside the objective. Pulling one of the four pink seeds breaks the ring and gives access to the other faces of the corner beds.

004 · Broken staircase

Revised · 13 → 11 input steps

Four interchangeable diagonal deliveries made the last two feel like cleanup. Three steps retain the staircase. The middle pair now has its own color and the last pair reverses direction.

005 · Unequal axes

Retained · 15 input steps

The teal pickup and bed share the short vertical axis. The four amber beds compete for seeds on the horizontal axis. This gives a second player an immediate, independent job.

006 · Loaded T

Retained · 2 input steps

Four arms can grab together from spawn, then plant together from the same square. The entire layout is legible as one cooperative action. Keep as the coordination benchmark.

007 · Return cargo

Revised · 18 → 14 input steps

The old banks sent amber cargo across three times. The shortened banks leave two outbound amber jobs and a teal return job. A second hand can make the return trip productive.

008 · Elbow plug

Revised · 24 → 15 input steps

The old elbow was recognizable, but its five pink deliveries outlasted the access puzzle. Three pink beds now form the elbow, with a seed at its tip and an amber side job.

009 · Inside the triangle

Revised · 21 → 11 input steps

The original triangular outline gave little guidance about which hand to use. The apex now offers a straight amber shot. The two base pickups face the other color’s bed across the rover.

010 · Missing tooth

Revised · 15 → 13 input steps

The original comb had four identical teal strokes. One missing tooth gives the amber job a place in the silhouette and breaks the repeated sequence.

011 · Three to two

Retained · 9 input steps

Three amber seeds can fill either of two beds. Its value is teaching that one seed can be left behind. Its open geometry makes it one of the least distinctive rooms on its own.

012 · Edge midpoints

Retained · 19 input steps

The four edge beds and corner seeds make the board easy to recognize. The actual task is conventional matching, so this works best as a spatial reading exercise.

013 · Hinged rail

Retained · 35 input steps

The pink seed is the hinge in an otherwise solid rail. The upward grab opens the route, but the pink bed sits below. The regrip has a clear purpose the player can anticipate.

014 · Cross-dock

Revised · 20 → 17 input steps

The old layout sent a whole packet of each color to a separate cluster. The new dock puts one amber seed near its destinations and keeps one short teal job across the room.

015 · Split diamond

Revised · 26 → 18 input steps

Eight beds made the outer silhouette more memorable than the last deliveries. Five retain the diamond, with a single teal apex and two pink side trips.

016 · Off-axis feed

Retained · 13 input steps

Four identical destinations can use five seeds. The outer cross is familiar, but the extra off-axis seed lets the team choose which arm to load.

017 · Unpick the weave

Revised · 45 → 17 input steps

The old weave was the most laborious route. The smaller patch keeps the first decision about which seed to pull. Four beds replace eight, shortening the cleanup after the patch opens.

018 · Slipped diagonal

Revised · 18 → 11 input steps

The old parallel diagonals were visually clear but played like scattered errands. Two teal pickups sit on a shifted diagonal below their beds. The pink pair on the top row offers a quick job before or after the diagonal crossing.

019 · Facing forks

Revised · 23 → 15 input steps

The old forks repeated their amber branches after the central decision. The two rare seeds now sit beside the rover and the two amber tips finish the shape.

020 · Living partition

Retained · 25 input steps

The mixed-color fence offers a consequential first choice. A pickup selects a color, opens a crossing, and changes which side of the remaining seeds the team can work from.

021 · Two hands, one trip

New · 3 input steps

Two hands pick up from opposite sides. A single upward move carries both loads into line with their beds for simultaneous planting.

022 · Turn the seed

New · 11 input steps

The supply and bed lie in opposing pockets. The pickup hand points the wrong way at delivery, and the open lower floor gives the team room to drop and regrip.

023 · Swap queue

New · 11 input steps

The nearer amber seed screens the teal seed. The tested route swaps cargo with the same hand, plants teal, then returns for amber.

024 · Crossed sockets

New · 8 input steps

Three hands can load at spawn, but the colors point at the wrong sockets. The rover has to find planting stances while already carrying several seeds.

025 · Dye line

New · 3 input steps

One amber seed, a teal cloud, and a teal bed make the conversion order visible along a single line. A short teaching room for the cloud interaction.

026 · Loaded lever

New · 15 input steps

One arm can press the lever while another takes the amber seed. The door opens the route to both beds, so the second trip uses the access created on the first.

027 · Tide gate

New · 10 input steps

The pulsing door offers a timed crossing and the open ends offer a detour. The tested route waited for the door while holding teal and carried it through.

028 · Two relays

New · 21 input steps

The horizontal pockets force an amber handoff, and the vertical pockets pose the same problem to the other pair of hands. Both can use the open center to stage cargo.

029 · Fold the fan

New · 16 input steps

Three hands collect from a fan around spawn. The wing beds accept their cargo from the sides. The central seed starts in the down hand and needs a regrip to reach the top bed.

030 · Choose the pigment

New · 11 input steps

Three amber seeds feed two amber beds and one teal bed. The cloud can convert one seed. The player chooses which amber seed to divert and which two to plant directly.
